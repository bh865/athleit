Your **family** holds your children and the adults who look after them. Adding a child first makes it easy to join a team or register them for a season.

## Add a child

<Steps>

1. Open **Family**.
2. Choose **Add a child** and enter their first name, last name and date of birth.
3. Optional: enter **Their own login email** if an older player will have their own account.

</Steps>

## Add another parent or guardian

Another adult can join your family with a **family code**. Choose **Join a family with a code** on their account and enter the code you share with them. Guardians can manage the children in the family.

<WhoCanDo capability="family.manage" label="Manage a family" />

<Callout kind="warning">
  Only share a family code with someone you trust to manage your children's information.
</Callout>

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Can a child have their own login?">
    Older players can have a login. Children under 13 do not have a login, so their guardians use
    Athleit on their behalf.
  </Q>
</FAQ>
