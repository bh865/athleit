The **Schedule** shows practices, games and other events for your team. Each event has an RSVP for each of your children.

## RSVP

<Steps>

1. Open the event from **Schedule** or from your Home screen.
2. Under your child's name, choose **Going** or **Can’t go**. **Maybe** is also available.
3. You can change your answer later. The coach sees the new answer straight away.

</Steps>

## Get the schedule in your calendar

The schedule can be added to your phone calendar as a calendar feed.

<Callout kind="note">
  Calendars refresh on their own schedule, so changes can take a few hours to appear in your
  calendar app. The Athleit schedule is always up to date.
</Callout>

## Who can do what

<WhoCanDo capability="schedule.rsvpOwn" label="RSVP" />
