Deleting your account cannot be undone.

## Before you delete

You cannot delete your account while you are the **only admin** of a team or club, the **only guardian** of children in a family, or while you have a **payment plan with installments still to pay**. The page lists what to transfer first. Make someone else an admin or guardian, or remove the team, club or children, then come back.

## Delete

<Steps>

1. Open **Account** and choose **Delete account**.
2. Read what is deleted and what is kept.
3. Type **DELETE** to confirm and choose **Permanently delete account**.

</Steps>

## What is deleted and what is kept

- **Deleted:** your profile and photo, notification settings and devices, sign-in methods and memberships.
- **Kept:** payment records clubs must keep, signed waivers, and messages you sent, shown as “Former member”.
- **Support requests** you sent to Athleit are anonymized and their attachments are deleted.
