Open **Account** and find notification settings. For each topic you can turn **push** and **email** on or off.

Topics include **Messages**, **Announcements**, **Schedule changes**, **Payments**, **Registrations and waivers** and, for admins, **Team billing**.

## Quiet hours

Choose a **From** time, an **Until** time and your **Timezone** to set quiet hours.

## Your devices

Your devices that receive push notifications are listed under **Devices**.

<Callout kind="note">
  Messages about a child can be sensitive, so a notification may show only that you have a new
  message and not the text.
</Callout>
