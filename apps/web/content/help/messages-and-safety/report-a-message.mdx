If a message worries you, you can report it. Anyone who can read a conversation can report a message that someone else sent.

<Callout kind="warning">
  If a child is in danger, call your local emergency number first. Reporting a message in Athleit is
  not an emergency service.
</Callout>

## Report a message

<Steps>

1. Open the conversation and find the message.
2. Open the message actions and choose **Report this message**.
3. Follow the prompts to send the report.

</Steps>

Team staff review the report. **The sender isn’t told who reported it.** Staff can hide a message, which removes its text for everyone, and can dismiss a report that needs no action.

## What you can and cannot see

You see messages from the time you joined a conversation. Conversations that include a child follow extra safety rules: a one to one conversation with a child is only possible with that child's guardian, and group conversations that include a child need two adults.

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Is this the same as contacting Athleit Support?">
    No. Reporting sends the message to your team's staff. To reach Athleit, use [Contact
    support](/help/contact).
  </Q>
</FAQ>
