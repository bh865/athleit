Before you can collect payments, a team admin connects a payout account. Money from payments goes to your bank account. Setup is a one-time step.

<WhoCanDo capability="payments.setup" label="Set up payouts" />

<Steps>

1. Open your team's **Payments** page and find **Payouts · Not set up**.
2. Choose **Set up payouts to start collecting**.
3. Follow the secure steps and enter your details. If something is missing, the page says **Add the missing details to turn payouts on**.
4. When setup is done the page says **Payouts are on**.

</Steps>

## Who pays the card fee

Under **Who pays the card fee by default**, choose whether the team absorbs it or **Families pay a service fee**. See [Why was I charged a service fee?](/help/a/why-was-i-charged-a-service-fee).

Coaches and managers can send requests once payouts are on, but only a team admin can set them up.
