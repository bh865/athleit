When your team allows it, you can split a request into 2 to 6 payments. If a plan does not fit the due date, only paying in full is offered, so nobody is locked out.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>

1. Open the request from **Pay**.
2. Under **How would you like to pay?**, choose a plan.
3. The first payment is taken now. The screen shows **Pay now** and when each later payment is due.

</Steps>

The total split evenly. Any leftover cents are added to the first payment.

## Automatic or manual later payments

You choose whether later payments are charged automatically to the card you use today, or whether you pay each one yourself and get a reminder before it is due.

<Callout kind="warning">
  Each installment is a separate card payment, so a fixed per-payment fee can apply to each one.
  Paying in full can cost slightly less. The checkout shows the total before you pay.
</Callout>

If a later payment fails, the plan stays open and the card is tried again. Your team can also remind you, waive an installment or move the due dates.
