Your team can send payment requests for fees, uniforms and more. They appear under **Pay** and on your Home screen.

## Pay

<Steps>

1. Open **Pay** and choose the request, or select several to pay together.
2. Check the total. It shows the amount, and any fee as its own line.
3. Choose **Continue to payment** and enter your card details.
4. Wait for **Confirming your payment** to finish and do not close the window.

</Steps>

If it takes longer than usual, you can close the window. You will not be charged twice, and your receipt is sent once the payment is confirmed.

## Find your receipt

Receipts are listed under **Pay**. Each has a receipt ID you can copy. If a payment is refunded, the receipt shows **Refunded** or **Partially refunded**.

<Callout kind="note">
  Athleit does not store your card number. Payments are processed by Stripe.
</Callout>
