At checkout the total can have up to three lines: the **amount** the team asked for, an **Athleit charge** when one applies, and a **service fee**.

## What the service fee is

A service fee covers the card processing fee. Each team chooses who pays it by default. Under **Families pay a service fee**, the fee is shown to you as its own line. Otherwise the team absorbs it and you pay only the amount requested.

## Refunds

When a payment is refunded, what is returned depends on the team's refund choices for the charge and for the service fee. Your receipt shows what was refunded.

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Can I avoid the fee?">
    The fee is part of the card payment. Ask your team admin whether they can take payment another
    way, for example offline, and record it for you.
  </Q>
</FAQ>
