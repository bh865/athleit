When you register a child for a season, the team can require one or more waivers.

<Steps>

1. Start the registration. The waivers appear after your answers, with the count, for example **Waiver 1 of 2**.
2. Read each waiver and sign it.
3. When the last one is signed you see **All waivers are signed. Thank you.**

</Steps>

You can find your registrations and what is signed under **Registrations**.

<Callout kind="note">
  Signed waivers are kept as a record, even if you later delete your account.
</Callout>
