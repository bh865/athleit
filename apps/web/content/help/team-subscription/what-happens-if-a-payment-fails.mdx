## A team subscription payment fails

All team admins are told. The team keeps full access for a grace period of <Live value="grace_days" />, with a banner that asks an admin to update the card.

<Steps>

1. Open the team's **Billing** page.
2. Choose **Update payment method** and fix the card.
3. When a payment succeeds, the team returns to normal straight away.

</Steps>

If the grace period ends without a payment, access becomes restricted. Messages, the schedule and existing payments keep working, and nothing is deleted.

Coaches and managers see that there is a problem and are asked to tell a team admin.

## A family payment fails

If your card is declined, the screen says **Your card was declined** and you were not charged. Try another card. If the connection drops, you are only charged if a receipt appears.

<Callout kind="note">
  If you see **We couldn’t verify your card with your bank**, you were not charged. Try again or use
  another card.
</Callout>
