<WhoCanDo capability="billing.manage" label="Manage the subscription" />

## Update the card

<Steps>

1. Open your team's **Billing** page.
2. Choose **Update payment method** (or **Manage payment method**).
3. Update the card in the secure portal and return to Athleit.

</Steps>

## Cancel

Choose **Cancel subscription** and confirm. Your team keeps full access until the end of the period you already paid for, and the page shows when it ends.

## Resume

Before the period ends you can resume the subscription so it carries on. After it has ended, subscribe again to start a new subscription.

<Callout kind="note">
  Cancelling never ends service early, and nothing in your team is deleted.
</Callout>
