A team subscription is paid by a team admin. The team starts with a free trial of <Live value="trial_days" />, then the price is <Live value="price" />.

## Subscribe

<Steps>

1. Open your team and go to **Billing**.
2. Choose **Subscribe** and follow the secure checkout.
3. If your trial still has more than two days left, you are not charged until the trial would have ended.

</Steps>

No card is needed until you subscribe.

<WhoCanDo capability="billing.manage" label="Subscribe, change the card or cancel" />

Coaches and managers can see the status (for example when the trial ends) but not invoices or card details. Families and club admins do not see billing.

<Callout kind="note">
  If your trial has less than two days left, the first charge happens a little after the original
  trial end date. The screen tells you the new date.
</Callout>

<FAQ>
  <Q q="What stops if the trial ends and nobody subscribes?">
    Access becomes restricted: new payment requests, registrations, invites and join approvals
    pause. Messages, the schedule, RSVPs and existing payments keep working, and nothing is deleted.
  </Q>
</FAQ>
